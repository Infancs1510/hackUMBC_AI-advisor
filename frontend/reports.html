About these reports

Built from the HackUMBC synthetic dataset — campus IDs only, no names — plus appointments, meeting requests, course plans and reviews recorded in this app. They are not official university records and don't come from a student information system.

Salaries are nominal dollars, not inflation-adjusted. Course demand is estimated from remaining requirements and prerequisites, not from registration.
