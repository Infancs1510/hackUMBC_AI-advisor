How plans are checked

  • check_circlePrerequisites must be met by completed courses or ones the student is taking now (including transfer credit implied by later courses).
  • blockBlocked: already completed, in progress, missing a prerequisite, or not in the catalog. Blocked plans can't be approved.
  • warningWarnings: not usually offered in Spring, or a credit load under 12 or over 18.
  • eventMeeting first: academic warning or probation, or required courses that can't all fit before expected graduation. These never batch-approve.
